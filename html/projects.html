Rapid Games

October 2025

Rapid Games is a free online gaming platform featuring a hand-picked collection of browser games curated from top sites like CrazyGames.com and Poki.com. Created by young developers passionate about gaming, it offers an extensive variety of action, puzzle, sports, and racing games all in one convenient location. The site prioritizes simplicity, speed, and fun, providing an intuitive search function to help you quickly discover your favorite games across multiple genres. Built with passion and dedication by developers Purv (Me) , Ridit , and Devansh . Rapid Games is thoughtfully designed to deliver an enjoyable, accessible gaming experience for players of all interests and skill levels, whether you're looking for casual fun or competitive challenges.

My Portfolio

March 2025
