My Books


I have written three super-interesting books on a platform known as Bribooks . I have both, fiction and non-fiction books. They are of various topics ranging from Science Fiction to Technology.

Dubai and a Futuristic City


This book is has 2 sections the first section is about when me and my friends get teleported to Dubai while playing hide and seek and we explore the city and the second section is when I get teleported to a futuristic city yet again but this time alone. Dubai and a Futuristic City is my favourite book personally. It is divided into two sections. The first section is more like a travelogue about my experience when I went to Dubai. It talks about monuments like the Burj Khalifa and the Museum of Future. It is based on my personal experiences and some of fellow travellers. On the other hand, the second section of it: A futuristic city. This completely of the first section, it is pure science fiction. It includes an object-maker that can build anything you want and a flying taxis that can take you wherever you want. This book is truly a testament of what humans can think of when they try.
