Purv's top favourite food items

Margarita Pizza:

Margherita pizza, a Neapolitan classic, celebrates simplicity with its three primary ingredients: tomatoes, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil. The dough, thin and chewy, serves as a canvas for a vibrant tomato sauce made from ripe, crushed tomatoes. Fresh mozzarella, creamy and soft, is generously layered across the sauce, followed by fragrant basil leaves that add a burst of herbaceous flavor. A drizzle of extra virgin olive oil completes this iconic dish, creating a harmonious blend of flavors and textures that epitomizes the essence of Italian cuisine.

Chocolate Brownie:

A decadent chocolate brownie is a rich, fudgy treat that satisfies any sweet tooth. Its dense, moist texture is achieved through a perfect balance of butter, sugar, cocoa powder, and eggs. The top is often crisp and crackly, while the interior remains soft and gooey. With every bite, the intense chocolate flavor envelops the taste buds, leaving a lingering sweetness that is both comforting and indulgent. Whether enjoyed warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or cold as a standalone dessert, a chocolate brownie is a timeless classic that never fails to delight.
